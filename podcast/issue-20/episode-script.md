## 02-breaking

Case definition: the trolley, silver, slightly bent front wheel, first noticed outside number 14 a fortnight ago. Moved seven times since by seven different neighbours, to seven different collection points. Returns to the same corner outside number 14 within two days, every time, with no visible assistance.
