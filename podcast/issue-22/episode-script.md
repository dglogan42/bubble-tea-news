## 02-breaking

Our top story. Manuka Terrace has an outbreak. (beat) Of hopscotch. Chalk grids, numbered one to ten, are appearing overnight outside driveways. Fourteen driveways so far. Two more every night. (beat) Three residents have been seen hopping square ten on the way to the car. Nobody admits to owning chalk. (beat) Which is exactly what someone who owns chalk would say. One grid has a tiny boba cup drawn in the home square. Investigators are calling it a signature. I'm calling it a cry for attention. And honestly? It's working.

## 04-sip-rule

That's not a drink. That's evidence.
